## Scope and replacement checks

- Teachers: assigned section(s); class grants expand to the class sections. A school-wide grant covers every section only for the permissions granted by that role. Unrelated school-wide HR or transport grants do not expand teaching or student-record scopes. Unsupported campus/department/custom and incompatible role/scope grants are rejected until their boundaries are implemented. Self and linked-child roles do not become unrestricted section scopes.
- Parents: verified linked children only. Students: own permitted records. Finance, HR, transport, library and auditor capabilities follow their individual action grants above.
- Access checker: `access.roles.manage` or read-only `access.roles.inspect` required on both UI and API. It reads actual database grants, does not impersonate the selected person, and blocks grants for disabled accounts.
- Replacement recommendations: active school teacher role plus linked staff account, verified subject/grade profile, no recorded absence/approved leave, no overlapping effective timetable lesson, and weekly workload below the profile limit. Unmarked attendance is explicitly identified for manual availability confirmation.
- Assignment: selected date must match the lesson weekday and academic year; API repeats eligibility checks under a school assignment lock and audits the result. No permanent user role is granted. Dated substitutes can submit period attendance for their exact assigned lesson through the period endpoint; general daily registers retain the usual section scope.
- Advanced attendance reads and period writes now enforce section scope. Period writes derive teacher/substitute identity from the scheduled lesson and reject forged identities.

## Remaining review items

This mapping is not a claim that every workflow has passed user acceptance. Run teacher, parent, student, principal and administration scenarios with school-approved pilot accounts before release. Institution-approved duty separation, dedicated vice-principal/HOD policy, substitute acknowledgement/cancellation screens, absence-triggered automatic suggestions, term/calendar exceptions and a substitute-facing period register are follow-up features. Existing sensitive-data separation and two-person approval controls remain relevant.
