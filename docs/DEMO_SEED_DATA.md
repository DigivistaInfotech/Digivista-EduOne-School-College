## 4. CBSE Compliance Features in Test Data

1. **CBSE Registration Numbers:** Automatically generated for all students in Grades 9–12 formatted as `CBSE/2026/G10/XXXX`.
2. **Indian Phone Numbers:** Formatted in canonical E.164 / normalized standard (e.g. `+91 98765 XXXXX`).
3. **Vehicle Registrations:** Authentic Indian RTO and Ministry of Road Transport & Highways Bharat (BH) series format.
4. **Attendance Shortage Register:** Pre-seeded students below 80% with medical certificates and Principal condonation notes under Rule 14.2.
